# LocalRelay seed dataset card

Version: seed-1. Origin: developer-authored synthetic templates generated by ml/seed.py. 336 messages in 84 synthetic families; all `source: synthetic`, all `reviewStatus: unreviewed`. No actual human author cohorts, interviews, consented operator examples or independent test messages. All content is newly authored project fixture data under the repository MIT licence.

Fields: id, authorGroup, paraphraseFamily, source, text, intent, requirements, expectedFields, expectedClarifications, expectedUnsupportedDetails, reviewStatus. Expected field/clarification annotations in this training corpus are empty rather than invented ground truth; parser semantics are evaluated separately in exact unit fixtures. Unsafe/extra clause annotations are visible synthetic text. Synthetic intent families deliberately use many vocabulary styles; variations share family IDs. Requirement clauses repeat across families; report this limitation.

Counts/splits/hashes are preserved in evaluation.json, splits.json and model metadata. Split components share either author or paraphrase family, keeping variants together. Labels are seed development annotations only and need review. Binary thresholds are tuned on development examples, test results are frozen and below intent target.

Known limitations: small corpus, no natural distribution, repeated conditions, lexical ambiguity, annotation noise, no author independence, fixed future dates in training (classification ignores date truth), very few test positives for vegetarian/health/extra heads. No claims about performance on Bangladeshi tourism enquiries. Human data must be explicitly consented/licensed, deidentified and independently grouped before commit.
